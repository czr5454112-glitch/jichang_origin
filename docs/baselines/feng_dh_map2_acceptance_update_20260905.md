# User clarification: proximity is a scientific review, not a rigid cutoff

After the repaired-control, retained-boundary V2 and outlet-gating V3 results,
the user clarified: “性能接近或者劣于冯cie论文中的数据即可，不用卡的太死，
但是最好也不要劣化太多”. This supersedes the hard advancement interpretation
of the initial 5%/10% thresholds. It does not change any completed observations.

Use the historical numbers as reference scale. A defensible reconstruction
that is close or moderately slower can advance, after reviewing mean, maximum,
per-OD free-flow and congestion behavior, conservation, and implementation
evidence together. The old percentages remain descriptive diagnostics only.
No new percentage is represented as a user-specified condition.

The retained-boundary V2 mean of about 1066 s versus 266 s is a large degradation
and fails this qualitative requirement too. A modestly slower result must not
be rejected simply for missing a previous exact percentage. Numerical proximity
alone still does not identify unavailable source semantics or justify choosing
an artificial delay to weaken DH. G31 results are not used in this assessment.

The map2-first condition remains active: no new Nanning or expanded experiment
matrix until the whole-day map2 reconstruction receives this combined review.
Previously completed matrices and source identities are retained as historical
versioned results, not silently promoted to the new reconstruction.
