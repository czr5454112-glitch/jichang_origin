# G4IRSF23 new ideas and evidence ledger

This ledger separates measured facts from hypotheses. An idea becomes a project conclusion only after its stated test passes. The default preference is the smallest local mechanism that advances decentralized `MOVE/WAIT` control; extra planners, supervisors, global scans, model families, and defensive machinery require positive evidence.

## New simplification insights to retain

1. **Action identity before learning.** A model is irrelevant until `ADMIT_NOW` and same-front one-opportunity HOLD are proven to be two real native outcomes from the same legal checkpoint.
2. **One hold bit is enough.** The minimal runtime state is the local held bag plus the natural-opportunity expiry (and the existing once-only marker). It should reuse the existing checkpoint state and add no integrity/hash subsystem, scheduler, reservation table, or other machinery.
3. **Forced A0 is the safety and complexity boundary.** After one treatment HOLD, normal A0 makes the next decision. This prevents learned retry loops and keeps ownership local.
4. **Observational hotspots only choose where to experiment.** Large Source deltas do not reveal the correct action or prove recoverable gain. This distinction should remain explicit in every report.
5. **Scale claims follow evidence, not ambition.** Prefer a completed 256-pair pilot with honest no-go over a large, weakly identified campaign. Advance to formal learning and 1x/2x only on preregistered gates.
6. **Externality fallback stays one hop.** The node-16 neighborhood needs only the alternate edge's current target queue (`>=16`, split into `q16_23/q24_31/q32_plus`). Two-hop pressure added no selected-population distinction in the frozen plan, so it is removed from selection, binning, thresholds, and the held-out signature; the same 256 exact target identities are retained for a clean evidence comparison.
7. **Tail gates should match the decision scale.** Externality system safety uses p95/p99 (each at most +1 ms) plus the deadline-miss gate. Per-action raw-bag max delta remains visible in CSV/summary/report, but is diagnostic only: one extreme bag cannot silently veto an otherwise p95/p99-safe action or change continuation.
8. **Keep proven outcomes, not an unused Route shortcut.** The real precursor campaign executes the frozen G22 profile, so a G23-only same-event/terminal Route baseline reuse path had no production caller. Historical compact evidence remains usable because its checkpoint-continuation outcomes were equivalence-audited and its execution provenance is stated in the report; the shipped runtime keeps ordinary per-target G22 semantics instead of retaining dead cache/counter branches.

## Source exact result and required pivot

The completed 2x exact pilot separates mechanism success from optimization success:

- **Mechanism validated:** all 256 unique Source groups produced a same-state, exactly-one-action `ADMIT_NOW` versus same-front one-opportunity HOLD mutation. All observed contracts and safety gates passed; there were no repeated HOLDs, unsafe entries, unresolved deadlocks, global scans, stored future routes, or runtime full-A* calls.
- **System support failed:** all 176 H_system outcomes were complete (128 block 7, 48 block 8), but 173 were neutral and only three were weak diagnostic improvements of about 4.837 ms. No row reached the 10 ms usable threshold, block 8 produced no beneficial row, and zero rows were promotion eligible.
- **Source decision:** `TARGETED_SOURCE_NO_SUPPORT` and `SOURCE_ADMISSION_SELECTOR_NO_GO`. Do not train a threshold, linear model, or tiny MLP on these labels, and do not spend 1x/2x/4x runtime on a Source closed loop that has no pilot support.
- **Keep a NO-GO path physically small:** because no Source selector was authorized or executed, its unused offline trainer and trainer-only tests are not shipped. The compact pilot evidence and decision status remain authoritative; at runtime, abstention still means the unchanged exact A0 action.
- **Next seam:** activate the preregistered precursor Route contingency. Reuse the existing local Route decision and exact bounded horizons; test one alternative legal next edge or WAIT without adding a new event loop, global MAPF planner, or centralized supervisor.

This result adds a useful design distinction: **100% action applicability is not evidence of causal leverage**. A local action can be perfectly implementable and safe while merely reallocating one millisecond of wait with no promotable system benefit. The simplest response is to move to an earlier real branching decision, not to increase model capacity at node 52.

## Precursor Route Pilot result and bounded next step

The completed precursor Route Pilot found a real local action seam, but not enough transferable support to promote it:

- **Exact mechanism evidence:** 512 unique groups produced 1,024 exact execution pairs, and all 512 groups changed the Route action. All H_bag groups completed; the fixed H_system panel completed for 256 groups, with each system branch also supplying the identical action's H_bag evidence.
- **Insufficient support:** only six groups were system-beneficial and promotion-eligible. All six were in block 7, while block 8 produced zero promotion-eligible groups. This misses both preregistered support requirements (at least 16 total and at least four in block 8), so the Pilot decision is `NO_GO_PRECURSOR_PILOT_SUPPORT` despite coverage, action-change, safety, tail, deadline, and four-strata checks passing.
- **Benefit and fairness are orthogonal:** all six beneficial actions made the treated bag slower than the strict +1 ms no-delay diagnostic, yet remained individually fair because their measured delay stayed within the pre-action deadline headroom, the treated bag completed by its deadline, and system deadline misses did not increase. Therefore system benefit must still come from H_system, while individual fairness must still be checked from H_bag; neither label can stand in for the other.
- **Strict no-delay remains diagnostic:** zero actions passed the strict +1 ms direct-delay diagnostic. Treating that diagnostic as the only fairness gate would discard all six headroom-safe system benefits; treating system benefit alone as fairness would hide their direct cost. The existing two-label rule is the smallest honest boundary.
- **Formal expansion completed without support:** the bounded Formal protocol reused the Pilot's exact 512-group prefix and added 1,536 non-overlapping H_bag-only groups. All 2,048 H_bag groups completed, while the sparse H_system panel remained the preregistered 256 groups. It retained only six fair promotions and zero in block 8, so the 16-total / 4-block-8 support gate failed.
- **Externality fallback completed without support:** the preregistered one-hop neighborhood evaluated only the existing node-16 `17 -> NEXT_EDGE 21` action at H_system. It did not add a planner, supervisor, reservation layer, WAIT action, H_bag replay, or model family.

The Pilot claim is deliberately narrow: the tested precursor action is native, safe, and sometimes system-beneficial, but the Pilot did not establish the minimum total or block-8 support needed for learning or deployment. The completed Formal and externality evidence below keeps the same narrow claim boundary rather than generalizing it to every local action.

## Formal and one-hop externality result

The completed Formal and externality stages close this G23 branch without increasing runtime complexity:

- **Formal evidence stayed at six positives:** the exact Formal execution covered 2,048 groups and 4,096 execution pairs, with 2,048 complete H_bag groups and the preregistered sparse 256-group H_system panel. It found six fair promotion groups, all in block 7, versus requirements of at least 16 overall and four in block 8. The decision is `NO_GO_PRECURSOR_FORMAL_SUPPORT`; the separate tiny-MLP unlock also remains false, so no model training or scale campaign is justified.
- **The externality panel was executed as planned, without replacement sampling:** all 256 frozen one-hop target identities were attempted. Native execution applied the requested action in 243 groups and safely abstained in 13 groups because the action precondition did not hold. The action-changing rate was 0.949219, above the inherited 0.80 applicability gate. A recognized guard abstention is completed applicability evidence, not an action-changing certificate or a zero-effect label; effect, fairness, and held-out calculations therefore use only the 243 applied pairs.
- **Local benefits were too sparse:** 17 applied groups were both system-beneficial and individually fair, below the required 20. They covered 10 block-by-pressure cells, so breadth alone passed the three-cell gate, but it did not rescue the missing count support.
- **The held-out direction failed:** discovery selected `q24_31`. In held-out blocks 26-29, that bin had 46 applied groups and three system-beneficial groups, but its treatment-minus-baseline mean and median raw-bag deltas were +3.015333 s and +2.411616 s. Both have the harmful sign, so the preregistered held-out signature failed.
- **The panel was harmful on average:** across all 243 applied pairs, the raw-bag mean-delta distribution had mean +3.472750 s and median +2.152940 s. Only 17 pairs met the system-benefit definition. System safety still uses p95/p99 plus deadline misses; the per-pair raw-bag maximum remains a visible diagnostic and is not a hidden hard gate.
- **Externality decision:** coverage, recognized-outcome, action-change-rate, and fair-cell gates passed; the 20-positive and held-out-signature gates failed. The decision is `NO_GO_EXTERNALITY_NEIGHBORHOOD_SUPPORT`.

The evidence-bounded conclusion is `TESTED_SEAM_LOCAL_ACTION_CEILING`. It applies only to the tested node-52 same-front one-opportunity HOLD, the node-28/node-39 precursor alternatives, and the node-16 `17 -> 21` one-hop externality action under the frozen G22 profile. It is not evidence that every one-step action, every local coordination rule, MAPF-style decentralization, or the broader framework must fail. The simplest next-step rule is to stop adding selectors and model capacity to these seams; any future branch should first preregister a different native local interaction and prove causal support before adding learning or scale runs.

## Rejected or prohibited shortcuts

- Do not call I1 queue swapping a same-front HOLD.
- Do not count an A0 physical block, repeated retry, proposal, or telemetry row as a committed treatment.
- Do not compare F2 and HCA* across unmatched THT denominators.
- Do not invent HCA* 2x/4x results, substitute v2-safe/static A*, or copy the 1x value.
- Do not add a centralized supervisor, full-route replanner, global queue scan, future reservation table, repeated learned HOLD, or broad defensive/hash work without a demonstrated failure that requires it.
